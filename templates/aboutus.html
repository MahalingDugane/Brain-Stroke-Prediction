Importance of Stroke Awareness

Understanding stroke symptoms and risk factors can help people recognize warning signs and seek medical attention quickly.

What Is a Brain Stroke?

A brain stroke occurs when the blood supply to part of the brain becomes blocked or when a blood vessel in the brain breaks.

Brain cells require oxygen and nutrients from blood to function correctly. When blood flow is interrupted, brain cells can become damaged.

Early medical treatment is important because a stroke can cause serious and long-term health complications.

Types of Brain Stroke

Stroke can happen in different ways. The major types include ischemic stroke and hemorrhagic stroke.

🩸

Ischemic Stroke

An ischemic stroke occurs when a blood clot or another blockage reduces blood flow to the brain. It is the most common type of stroke.

🧠

Hemorrhagic Stroke

A hemorrhagic stroke occurs when a blood vessel in the brain breaks and causes bleeding in or around the brain.

🔴

Thrombotic Stroke

A thrombotic stroke happens when a blood clot develops inside an artery that supplies blood to the brain.

🩺

Embolic Stroke

An embolic stroke happens when a clot or other material travels through the bloodstream and blocks an artery supplying the brain.

Common Warning Signs

Stroke symptoms can appear suddenly. Seek emergency medical help immediately if stroke symptoms occur.

Sudden Severe Headache

A sudden and severe headache may occur, particularly with other warning signs.

Vision Problems

Sudden difficulty seeing in one or both eyes may be a warning sign of stroke.

Difficulty Swallowing

Some people may experience difficulty swallowing or problems controlling their mouth muscles.

Face Drooping

One side of the face may droop or feel numb. Uneven facial movement can be a warning sign.

Arm Weakness

Sudden weakness or numbness in one arm or one side of the body can be a warning sign.

Speech Difficulty

Sudden difficulty speaking, understanding speech, or communicating may indicate a stroke.

Stroke Risk Factors

Several health and lifestyle factors can increase the risk of stroke. Regular health checkups are important.

  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes
  • High cholesterol
  • Smoking
  • Excess body weight
  • Physical inactivity
  • Heart disease
  • Family history of stroke
  • Excessive alcohol consumption
  • Increasing age

Stroke Prevention

Healthy lifestyle habits and regular medical checkups can help manage several stroke risk factors.

🥗

Healthy Diet

Eat a balanced diet with vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and appropriate portions.

🏃

Regular Exercise

Maintain regular physical activity according to your health condition and doctor's advice.

🩺

Health Checkups

Monitor blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, and other important health factors.
